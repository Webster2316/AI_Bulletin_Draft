Event Details

Developments in the marine fuels supply chain are changing how shipping operations are carried out. As the adoption of alternative marine fuels such as biofuels, methanol and ethanol grows, alongside the digitalisation of the bunkering process through electronic Bunker Delivery Notes (e-BDNs), shipowners and operators are navigating new operational, legal and insurance considerations. These developments are bringing new considerations for businesses as they adapt to evolving fuel practices and digital bunkering processes.

Unpack some of these considerations at the upcoming Maritime Conversations@SSA titled “Marine Fuels & e-BDN: Practical, Legal & Insurance Perspectives”, which will take place on Monday 14 September 2026. This session is jointly organised by our Marine Fuels and the Legal & Insurance Committee.

Date: 14 September 2026

Time: 10.00am – 12.00pm

Venue: SSA Office, Boardroom, 4 Battery Road, #28-01, Bank of China Building, Singapore 049908

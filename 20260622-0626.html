[For BCO's Inputs] Proposal to Augment Bunkering Ops with Video Surveillance Technology onboard Bunker Tankers

Main document link

Committee 1

⚠ Deadline: 6 July 2026

Action Required

BCOs are strongly encouraged to provide feedback and comments on the proposed specifications and the implementation timeline. Any questions, refer to Shuyi@ssa.org.sg

Background

Following up on the initiative shared at the Marine Fuel Forum 2026, MPA would like to seek views and inputs with regard to leveraging Video Surveillance Technology to improve bunker operations in Singapore port.

The initiative aims to address challenges faced by BCOs in the current landscape. BCOs currently rely heavily on physical attendance onboard to monitor vessel conditions and conduct audits/checks, where the lack of visibility of live onboard conditions may hinder timely interventions and identification of non-compliance or unsafe practices. This initiative may also reduce the burden on crew during emergencies (i.e. spills, collision, etc).

MPA is seeking BCO views on the following proposed specifications for the Video Surveillance Technology:

  • Weather resistance
  • Minimum 1080p Full HD video quality
  • Camera clarity maintained in dawn/dusk conditions
  • Continuous recording capability (24/7 coverage)
  • Minimum 30 days storage capacity
  • Coverage of Port & Starboard side and stern of the bunker tanker
  • Live footage with real-time transmission capability

MPA is considering an implementation period of 1 year upon finalisation of specifications, which would be incorporated into the Standards for Port Limit Bunker Tanker.

Rajah & Tann Singapore LLP Update: PDPC Proposes New Advisory Guidelines on Use of Personal Data in Generative AI

Document link

Committee 1 Committee 2

⚠ Deadline: 1 July 2026

Action Required

Members are requested to review the proposed new advisory guidelines on the use of personal data in Generative AI and provide comments or feedback to anis@ssa.org.sg, if any.

Background

On 2 June 2026, the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) issued Proposed Advisory Guidelines on Use of Personal Data in Generative AI, clarifying how the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 applies to key data protection issues where Gen AI development and deployment involves personal data. Areas covered include:

  • The collection and use of personal data to develop Gen AI models
  • The allocation of data protection responsibilities across the Gen AI cycle
  • The handling of individuals' requests concerning the processing of their personal data for Gen AI

PDPC is conducting a public consultation until 1 July 2026. Read more here.

CE(26)03 - Draft Submission to the CCC Sub-Committee on Low-Flashpoint Oil Fuels

Main document link  |  Another link

Technical Marine Fuels

⚠ Deadline: Monday, 29 June 2026

Action Required

Members are requested to review the draft submission attached at Annex A and provide comments and suggestions by Monday 29 June 2026 to anis@ssa.org.sg, if any.

Background

The ICS Secretariat has prepared a draft submission to CCC 12 on the development of interim guidelines for ships using low-flashpoint oil fuels. The submission supports the adoption of the "fuel safe" machinery space concept, which applies the double barrier principle to safely contain fuel leakages and manage risks associated with ignitable fuel vapours. The draft has been developed following the completion of the Correspondence Group's work and seeks to address outstanding safety concerns that have prevented finalisation of the guidelines.

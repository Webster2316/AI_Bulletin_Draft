Event Details

Developments in the marine fuels supply chain are changing how shipping operations are carried out. As the adoption of alternative marine fuels grows alongside the digitalisation of the bunkering process through electronic Bunker Delivery Notes (e-BDNs), shipowners and operators are navigating new operational, legal and insurance considerations.

Unpack some of these considerations at the upcoming Maritime Conversations@SSA titled “Marine Fuels & e-BDN: Practical, Legal & Insurance Perspectives”.

Date: 14 September 2026

Time: 10.00am – 12.00pm

Venue: SSA Office, Boardroom, 4 Battery Road, #28-01, Bank of China Building, Singapore 049908

Programme Topics

Adoption of Alternative Marine Fuels

  • Key developments and updates, including practical considerations in adopting alternative marine fuels
  • Common questions and misconceptions, as well as potential emerging issues
  • The status of electronic Bunker Delivery Notes (e-BDN) in Singapore, including practical considerations for industry adoption and operational or commercial issues for shipowners and other stakeholders

e-BDN Status and Legal Implications

  • Legal considerations surrounding electronic documentation, electronic signatures and authentication utilised in the e-BDN process
  • Key legal and insurance considerations that members should understand as bunkering documentation becomes increasingly digital
  • Other emerging legal or insurance-related developments relevant to shipowners and maritime stakeholders

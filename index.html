Scaling AI from awareness to practical adoption across the maritime industry
1. Awareness

Emerging AI Use Cases Across the Maritime Industry

As organisations gain confidence through early AI initiatives, attention is increasingly shifting towards scaling successful use cases across the business. Rather than viewing AI as a standalone technology, organisations are exploring how it can be embedded into existing workflows to improve productivity, reduce repetitive tasks and support more informed decision-making.

Examples of AI applications currently being explored include:

•AI-assisted voyage planning and route optimisation
•Predictive maintenance using equipment and sensor data
•Cargo and logistics optimisation
•Port and terminal operations optimisation
•AI-powered safety monitoring and risk assessment
•Internal knowledge management and intelligent document search
•Fleet performance and emissions optimisation
•Autonomous vessel technologies and decision support

Maritime AI Readiness Assessment Ongoing

The Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore (MPA) is currently conducting the Maritime AI Readiness Assessment for the sea transport sector using the AI Readiness Index framework developed by AI Singapore.

The assessment aims to help companies:

•Understand their current AI readiness level
•Identify gaps in digital and AI capabilities
•Support future industry programme development

Call for Participation

Companies are encouraged to nominate a representative familiar with their organisation's technology adoption status (CTO, CIO, Digital Lead or Senior Management representative).

Assessment Link: go.gov.sg/maritime-airi

Building a Structured Maritime AI Learning Journey

SSA is currently curating a structured AI learning journey to help maritime companies navigate the growing number of AI tools, concepts and training programmes available.

The programme will progressively support organisations through three stages:

1.Awareness & Understanding
2.Skills Development & Experimentation
3.Operational Implementation & Adoption

Further training runs and AI-related learning activities will be shared progressively.

Common AI Adoption Challenges Observed

As organisations progress beyond pilot projects, several operational challenges are emerging:

•Scaling successful pilots across multiple business functions
•Ensuring data quality and consistency for reliable AI outputs
•Defining governance, accountability and human oversight
•Integrating AI tools into existing workflows without adding complexity
•Measuring long-term business impact beyond initial productivity gains

Suggested Action This Month

Practical priorities identified through ongoing industry engagement include:

•Prioritise low-risk, high-impact AI use cases
•Map cross-departmental data dependencies
•Establish governance for AI-generated outputs
•Strengthen data quality and accessibility
•Standardise AI workflows and best practices across teams

Small, well-defined pilot projects often provide the strongest foundation for broader AI adoption across the organisation.
